How the estimate works

Before the dashboard, two people each spent at least one day per report finding data issues, discussing corrections and regenerating reports. Assuming an eight-hour day, that is at least 16 person-hours. For illustration, 10 programme/centre units × 4 quarterly cycles × (16 baseline hours − 1 assumed review hour) = 600 hours per year. This is a scenario, not measured savings. Reporting work can overlap, some areas may not report quarterly, and actual review, support and maintenance time must be measured. Staff Training and Corporate Counselling are shown in coverage but excluded from this quarterly estimate.

Shared dashboards make current figures, issues and supporting detail easier to review together. Salesforce reports and Excel remain useful; a custom system adds programme-specific rules, repeatable transformations, snapshots and tailored views, with ongoing engineering and hosting costs.
