Read the assumptions and limits

Example scenario: 12 cycles × (8 manual hours − 2 review hours) = 72 hours per year. Actual value depends on staff workflow, data quality, scope and adoption. The resume’s 80-hour estimate is separate and is not derived from this model. A Salesforce + Excel workflow can produce many reports, but cross-programme reconciliation, special rules, export formatting, historical snapshots and access-specific views can require additional configuration or manual work; actual limitations depend on the Salesforce configuration and licensing.

For event automation, a hypothetical 500 registrations/year × 3 minutes of manual handling is 25 hours. If exceptions and review take 8 hours, the scenario saves 17 hours/year. This excludes implementation, maintenance and software costs; validate volumes and handling times before claiming savings. Track reconciled registrations, exception rate, duplicate prevention, delivery success and cost per event as candidate metrics.
