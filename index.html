NatWest Markets · NUS capstone · 2022

ESG Disclosure Explorer

A banker preparing for a financing discussion can trace a company’s net-zero pledge to its emissions targets, reported progress and third-party verification.

Result

The original study compared nine climate indicators across 61 companies in four industries. The public explorer links each search result to its report page.

Try the Shell example ↗Read the 2030 target ↗Explore the full dashboard ↗
Project details for ESG Disclosure Explorer
  • The banking questionShell’s 2021 report shows emissions from its operations and purchased energy fell from 71 to 68 million tonnes of CO₂e. Asset sales contributed. How much of the decline came from changes at sites Shell kept operating?
  • Model evaluationThe capstone’s AdaBoost classifier flagged long-term net-zero pledges with 90.5% accuracy and 0.725 F1 on an internal test split of 1,003 labelled sentences.
  • Public demoSearch 3,035 pages from 27 public reports and check each passage against the original PDF.

Multilingual information retrieval · 2026

Scoop Wars

Given a news clue, the agent has two searches and 45 seconds to find the original reporting. Some clues alter a key fact, so the agent must decide when to leave the answer blank.

Result

On the same 15 practice clues, the starter submitted five wrong links. The revised agent submitted none in three repeat runs, scoring 14/15, 14/15 and 13/15.

Read the case study ↗View the results workbook ↗
Project details for Scoop Wars
  • Failure foundFor a deliberately altered vaccine-study clue, the starter’s own summary named a different beneficiary group from the clue. It still returned the article.
  • What changedThe agent checks decisive facts against article text, uses a second search when evidence is incomplete and leaves the card blank if a contradiction remains. Searches can run in English, Chinese or Indonesian.
  • EvaluationThe best recorded practice run scored 15/15 with a median answer time of 14.1 seconds. The practice audit records every answer, including abstentions, across five runs.
